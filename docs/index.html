Hi, I’m Ethan.

I’m a first-year PhD student at MIT working on energy access in fragile contexts — climate-affected islands, conflict zones, post-conflict settings, displacement camps. I’m interested in how ownership of energy and critical infrastructure can create economic and political sovereignty for communities.

(currently) I split time between Cambridge, Mass in the US and a never-ending renovation of an 18th-century farm in the rural Basque country of France my family and I bought together. I am a keen reader, motorcyclist, waterman, and (slow) developing engineer. In prior lives, I was a surf bum and English teacher in Latin America, a UN data scientist in South Asia, and ran M&E systems in East Africa for the randomistas during the peak of the RCT craze.

This website is part digital notebook / part commitment device but mostly a message in a bottle. If you’re in Cambridge or somewhere else in the world, please don’t hesitate to shoot me an email (echarr@mit.edu).
